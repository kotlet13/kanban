# Poziv za ikono Jivie

Vgrajeni imagegen, 7. oktober 2026; `transparent_background: true`.

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: production app-icon foreground for a new personal/family organizer named Jivie, pronounced ji-vee. No text.
Create ONE original, minimal, extremely well-resolved abstract emblem that intertwines three elements of life: EARTH, WATER, AIR. The elements must be abstract qualities of the geometry, not literal little pictograms. Earth is the weight and gently faceted grounded lower form; water is the broad fluid sweep weaving through it; air is the open ascending curve with generous breathing space. Fuse these into a single memorable, quietly lively silhouette, with a subtle woven over-under relationship and broad clean negative-space cuts. Three substantial, softly rounded strokes or organic bands, visually balanced with a slight asymmetry. Refined iconic geometry, not a decorative illustration.
Palette: a tightly related family of graphite-navy, restrained cobalt blue, and cool medium blue; keep contrast strong and avoid pale thin parts. Flat solid-color vector-like rendering with perfectly clean edges, no texture or lighting. The emblem must work at 32 pixels.
Composition: square 1024x1024 canvas, transparent background, mark centered, occupies about 62% of the width and height, with ample completely transparent margin on all sides for circular/squircle adaptive app masks. No rounded-square tile, no border, no shadow. Only the standalone emblem.
Avoid: literal mountains, leaves, droplets, clouds, animals, earth globes, gradients, 3D extrusion, Celtic triquetra, obvious trinity knot, trefoil flower, recycling arrows, pinwheel, Google Chrome resemblance or multicolor Google palette. No lettering, watermark, mockups, presentation boards or multiple options.
```
