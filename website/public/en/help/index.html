A small guide to getting started

Start with one useful thing. Connect the rest when you need it.

Do I need an account?

No. Choose local use and create your first task, plan or shopping list. Personal work is saved on your device, even without a connection.

Where do I find things?

Today shows what is coming up. Plans brings tasks, projects and events together. Shopping keeps your lists at hand. More opens finances, settings and options for connecting and sharing. On desktop, these areas appear in the sidebar.

How do I keep a backup?

Open backups in the app and create an encrypted portable copy. Keep its password safe: the backup cannot be restored without it. Before changing devices, check that you can restore the copy. Synchronization is not a backup.

How do I share with others?

Connect an HTTPS Kanboard server with the compatible FamilyHub plugin and its Native API enabled. The server operator prepares the first account or an invitation. Use your own account to join a shared home or project. A Kanboard server without the plugin does not provide these collaboration features. Connecting does not automatically upload your personal content.

What about notifications?

Local reminders need notification permission and a record available on the device. The shared notification centre refreshes when connected or synchronized. Remote phone notifications with the app closed are not automatically available for every self-hosted server.

What if I cannot sign in?

Check your server address and contact the server operator. Email password recovery requires server support and a previously verified email address. Never send your password or one-time codes to support.

How do I delete my account?

See the self-hosted account deletion process.
