A small guide to getting started

Start with one useful thing. Connect the rest when you need it.

Do I need an account?

No. Choose local use and create your first task, plan or shopping list. Personal work is saved on your device, even without a connection.

Where do I find things?

The four main destinations are Pulse, Planner, Space and More. Pulse shows today’s events, the next seven days, projects in progress and tasks. Planner contains Tasks, Calendar and Ideas. Space contains Projects and Finances, with Shopping, Garden and People where the selected space supports them. The garden keeps its own beds and seasons. More contains your account, space settings, app settings, backups and help. Android and iOS use the same four bottom tabs; desktop uses the same groups in a sidebar. Choose your space in the top selector. All combines the content you can access; choose a specific space before adding content. Members, invitations and sharing are managed in Space settings.

How do idea boards work?

Open Planner → Ideas. Add text, notes, photos or drawings to a board. Select a photo to attach markings and notes so they follow it when moved, resized or rotated. Drafts stay on your device; Save commits the board and prepares synchronization and an activity notification in a shared space. Private personal boards stay private. Photo availability is shown explicitly when a download is missing.

How do I keep a backup?

Open More → Backups and create an encrypted portable copy. Keep its password safe: the backup cannot be restored without it. The copy contains supported records, editable idea boards and downloaded idea photos; missing image downloads are marked incomplete. It excludes login sessions and unrelated server attachments and is not a complete server backup. Converting a restored server space to standalone local use leaves financial transfers and linked payments in the encrypted recovery archive; the local financial view remains incomplete. Before changing devices, check that you can restore the copy. Synchronization is not a backup.

How do I share with others?

Connect an HTTPS Kanboard server with the compatible FamilyHub plugin and its Native API enabled. The server operator prepares the first account or an invitation. Manage invitations in Space settings. The inviter enters your email address; open the emailed link, create an account or sign in, then accept the invitation separately. Joining a whole household or organization gives access to its shared content, finances and all existing and future projects. A project-only invitation gives access only to that project, its tasks, events, notifications and finances, without membership in its parent space. Within the shared scope, members can create, edit, delete, manage finances and invite or manage members. Ownership protections remain, and older sharing policies require an explicit transition preview. A Kanboard server without the plugin does not provide these collaboration features. Connecting does not automatically upload your personal content.

What about notifications?

Local reminders need notification permission and a record available on the device. The shared notification centre refreshes when connected or synchronized. In the current version, categories without saved preferences enable in-app and push notifications; email and sound are off. Explicit saved opt-outs remain. Remote phone delivery also needs compatible server configuration, explicit device registration and operating-system permission; defaults alone do not register a device. Mark all as read applies to the current filter and does not complete tasks or payments. Remote phone notifications with the app closed are not automatically available for every self-hosted server.

What if I cannot sign in?

Check your server address and contact the server operator. Email password recovery requires server support and a previously verified email address. Never send your password or one-time codes to support.

How do I delete my account?

See the self-hosted account deletion process.

Need a hand?

Tell us the app version, your operating system and what happened. Remove personal data from screenshots. support@jivie.app.
