Data and privacy

How data is handled in the local app, on your own server and on this website.

Personal use, on your device

You can use Jivie for personal tasks, plans, shopping and finances without an account. These records are stored on your device. The active local database does not have additional encryption provided by the app; protection also depends on your device and operating system.

Connecting your own server

For optional synchronization and sharing, you connect a compatible Kanboard server with the FamilyHub plugin. Signing in alone does not upload your personal content. You explicitly enable private synchronization; shared content is available according to the permissions of its space. The server processes account data, sessions, memberships, submitted content and technical data needed for operation and security. Its operator controls the deployment, logs, service providers and backups. TriparNA does not currently offer managed Jivie hosting.

Shared-content rules and reports

On a compatible FamilyHub server, you explicitly accept shared-content rules before publishing shared changes. The policy version and acceptance time are stored against your account on that server. Local personal use and private personal synchronization do not require this acceptance.

Before you submit a report about shared content or a member, you see the recipient, limited evidence and your explanation. The report goes to the administrator of your selected server; it is not automatically sent to TriparNA. Evidence contains the displayed text fields of one record or the reported member’s identity, rather than a complete workspace, sign-in secrets or linked private finances. Submitting a report does not itself remove content or membership.

The plugin removes details, evidence and review notes 180 days after a report is resolved, when server cleanup runs. Open reports remain for review; minimal process metadata remains. Account deletion removes its policy acceptance and associated reports and review notes as described in the deletion preview. Your server operator is responsible for actually reviewing reports, running cleanup, and handling server backups and logs.

Reminders and notifications

Local reminders use your device’s notification system and permissions. Remote notifications use Firebase Cloud Messaging and require compatible server configuration, explicit device registration and operating-system permission. In the current version, categories without saved preferences have push and in-app notifications enabled, with email and sound disabled. Your saved choices, including explicit opt-outs, take precedence. Older servers may use different category defaults. Delivery processes device tokens, technical SDK metadata and generic messages with record references; our push message does not contain task titles or financial amounts. This channel is not automatically available for arbitrary self-hosted servers. Android delivery has been tested on our test server; the iOS APNs connection is still being prepared. macOS currently has the in-app centre, without remote system push. Email delivery requires separate server configuration and may use a verified email address for security codes or enabled notifications.

Backups and exports

A portable .vsakdan backup is encrypted with a password you choose and saved to a location you select. It does not include login sessions or attachments and is not a complete server backup. When a restored server space is converted to standalone local use, transfers between financial accounts and linked payments are not activated as new local records. Their originals remain in the encrypted recovery archive, and the financial view is marked incomplete. The advanced JSON export is unencrypted. You control the copies you export and where they are stored.

Deleting data

Signing out does not delete your account or all local data. See account deletion for the self-hosted account process. Server deletion cannot recall copies other members already obtained or exports you saved elsewhere. Your server operator is responsible for the handling of its logs and backups. The deletion screen explains which active data is affected before confirmation.

This website

This static website does not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies set by its own code. Fonts, images and scripts are served from this website. The hosting infrastructure may process normal technical request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site. Entering a server address on the deletion page only prepares a link in your browser; this page does not check your account or send login credentials. Opening the link connects you to the server you selected.
