Local first

Start here.
Stay in control.

  1. 01
    Open it. Start locally.

    No account or server is needed for your personal plans, tasks, ideas, shopping and finances.

  2. 02
    Add your first little thing.

    A task for today, a note for a fresh idea, a plan for the weekend. Draft idea boards stay on your device until you save them.

  3. 03
    Keep a copy. Connect when it helps.

    Create an encrypted portable backup of supported records and downloaded idea photos. Choose a compatible server for optional syncing and sharing. See the guide for backup limits.
