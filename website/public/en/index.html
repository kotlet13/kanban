Stay in control.

  1. 01
    Open it. Start locally.

    No account or server is needed for your personal plans, tasks, shopping and finances.

  2. 02
    Add your first little thing.

    A task for today, a shopping list, a plan for the weekend. Your own content sets the pace.

  3. 03
    Connect when it makes sense.

    Choose a compatible server for syncing and sharing. You decide which personal content to transfer.
